I found you again; and after what felt like eternity, you as well. 

The rational tick from the cogs interlinked in my mind gear me away from any further investment in this dream. It is only a passing vision, birthed by the longing hand of my subconsciousness, and further spurred by the persistent loneliness that smothers the life I live.

It is only your tradition, which stands tall in the face of facts: a dream is no mere vision, it is a visit, by the souls we can no longer see nor hear nor feel. Even with ascension, you grace my sleep in reminder. Reassurance.

I saw a father, once thieved of power by a cancer none of us foresaw, bursting again with life. In the purple light of the moon, you were reignited, each martial pose you struck a demonstration of my hero's returned strength.

I saw a mother, once shackled by the burdens of ancestral duty, flying without weight. In the violet ray of the sun, you were unchained, the shore of the ocean you once feared beneath as you danced.

I know. There is no truth to this haze, only the wishes of a child for his loves. Prayers embracing my senses, to cloud my memory of how your hands felt as you took your final breaths. I wish despite my failings, regardless of where you have landed in the realm beyond, that my love can pierce the bounds of Earth, soar past the skies, and still find a home in your hearts. 

I am irrational. I am silly. I am naive. I am your son. And this is the only way I know how, to trace the ember you left in my heart. In a dance with fantasy and reality. In a swirl of bitter and sweet. In witness of my soul being torn and mended. Every cut, leaves a scarful reminder of when my heart was full, and the hope that it could be so again.

I am sorry, I woke up crying, still the crybaby you always known. I was sad you had to go. I was happy you stopped by.

We will meet again someday, plenty of stories in tow, on the other side of my lilac covered dreams.
