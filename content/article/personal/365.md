All year, I never found the words to put it all together.

It is cathartic (for me), when a collection of my own words, with a pinch of punctuation become more than the ink they are. This self satisfaction in expression, was my raft in the storm. 

How cruel is it, with you as the topic, that all year, nothing created in this journal ever touched the heights I aimed for. Too much to say. Too little skill to write it. No understanding of what I wanted. 

Through the pages and pages of letters and scribbles, we have come full circle to October 25th. I am here, but not my heart is not.

A mended soul is not delivered on a timeline. I know this already, a lesson learned 15 years ago, when we faced the same ghosts. But when drowning, how could I not reach? For any semblence of a savior? For the chance of moving on?

I don't know where I am. How much of this road is left. All I can do, maybe in vain, maybe simply to avoid the shame of being completely witless on this anniversary of fate, is to state the facts. To say what I felt. To say what I feel. 

At first, you were a sliver of ice on my senses, your abscence the only thing I see. I could not hear you. Could not touch you.

Then, an impossible dream. One where you would be on the other side of the door when I opened it, flashing your checkered toothy grin. Another, where you would peek your head around the corner, wondering what your son was cooking. 

Today, a longing for far away times. 

I miss lighting incenses for you at temples, counting all the shrines so you would have just enough to complete your prayers.  
I miss searching Spotify for Vietnamese bolero during our weekly drives, with you always asking if I listened to this, knowing damn well I don't without you.  
I miss seeing the moon just before they became half or full as a reminder to call you. "Mẹ, ngày mai ăn chay!" ("Mom, tomorrow eat vegetarian!").

I miss learning all the bits and bobs, that pieced together to be you. I miss having the special chance, to show you, that I love you.

After 365 days, this is where I am. Still hurting. Still missing you. But for what it is worth, I am not where I was.

And maybe, that is all there is. No passage with a bow wrapped ending. No poignant fulfilling sensation from skillfully penning feelings onto paper. Just an everlasting, bittersweet journey.

Only fitting, for our everlasting love. 

Rest easy Mẹ (Mom).

- Binh
