All year, I never found the words to put it all together.

It is cathartic (for me), when a collection of my own words (with a pinch of punctuation) become more than the ink they are. This self satisfaction in expression, was my raft in the storm. 

How cruel is it, with you as the topic, that all year, nothing created in this journal ever touched the heights I aimed for. Too much to say. Too little skill to write it. And no understanding of what I wanted. 

Through the pages and pages of letters and scribbles, we have come full circle to October 25th. I am here, but my heart is not.

A mended soul is not delivered on a timeline. I know this already, a lesson learned 15 years ago, when we faced the same ghosts. But when the misery drowned all else, I could not help but reach. 

I don't know where I am or how much of this road is left. All I can do, maybe in vain, maybe simply to avoid the shame of being completely witless on this anniversary of fate, is to state the facts. To say what I felt. To say what I feel. 

At first, you were a sliver of ice on my senses, your abscence the only thing I see, your silence all I can hear. My guilt numbed everything else.

Then, an impossible dream. The one where you would be on the other side of the door when I opened it, flashing your checkered toothy grin. Or the one, where I would see you peek your curious face around the kitchen corner as I cooked. Every single dream where you would be right here, even when I knew you could not.

Today, you are a longing for far away times. 

I miss lighting incenses for you at temples, counting all the shrines so you would have just enough to complete your prayers.  
I miss searching Spotify for Vietnamese bolero during our weekly drives, with you always asking if I listened to this, knowing damn well I don't without you.  
I miss seeing the moon as they waxed and waned to half or full as a reminder to call you. "Mẹ, ngày mai ăn chay!" ("Mom, tomorrow eat vegetarian!").
I miss learning all the bits and bobs, which pieced together to be you.  

But what I truly miss, is the chance to show you, that I love you.

After 365 days, this is where I am. Still hurting. Still the son you never stopped worrying about. But for what it is worth, I am not where I was. Somehow, even in the mess that is grief, I am somewhere else.

And maybe, that is all there is. No passage with a bow wrapped ending. No poignant fulfilling sensation from skillfully penning feelings onto paper. No yearly marker to cap the story. Just a constant, everlasting, bittersweet journey.

How fitting, for our love was, is, and will be.

Until we meet again, Mẹ (Mom).

- Binh
